Resolved by U4 (commit 6ddeb28): the first full render was moved out of synchronous
`Initialize()` into a one-shot `MainWindow.Opened` hook (`RenderInitial`), so controls are
measured/hit-testable on first paint — rows/badges render and toggles respond with no manual
Refresh. The wrong-badge symptom (3) was confirmed by-design (badge = on-disk injected mod ≠
configured mod), not a bug. Structural regression guard added in U6.1 (`--mac-uitest` asserts
first-paint rows>0 with no Refresh). Final visual confirmation is U6.3 (manual, user-run).
